The Featurep. 02

The best products start with watching closely and asking the right question.

I'm a researcher who works where people and unreleased technology meet. At AI Data Innovations, I've led around a thousand in-lab sessions on pre-release AR/VR products for a major tech client, from perception studies with professional musicians to daily analysis of session recordings that surfaces problems before launch.

I studied Informatics with a focus on data science at the University of Washington, graduating summa cum laude. On my own, I build data stories about real-life issues, starting with memory loss.

The thousand sessions taught me how people really meet new technology. Now I want to bring that understanding earlier, while a product is still being shaped and research can make the most difference.
