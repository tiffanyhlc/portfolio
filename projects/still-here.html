02 — The myth

"Everyone loses their memory when they get old."

Forgetting a name or misplacing keys is normal aging. Dementia is a disease, and most older adults never develop it. But because so many people believe it's normal, early signs get brushed off, help comes late, and caregivers carry it alone.
